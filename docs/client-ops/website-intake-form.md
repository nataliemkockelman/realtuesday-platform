# Website Intake — real Tuesday

Client-facing intake for a new website engagement. Use it as-is in a call, or
drop it into a Google Form / Typeform for the client to fill out on their own.
Questions marked **(required)** are the ones we can't scope without.

Fields in `[brackets]` are notes for us, not the client. Delete them before you
send it out.

---

## 0. The basics

1. Company or project name **(required)**
2. Your name and role **(required)**
3. Best email and phone **(required)**
4. Website of record, if one exists today (URL)
5. Who else needs to be in the loop on this? (name + role + email)

---

## 1. Your business, in your words

6. What does the business actually do? Say it the way you'd say it to a friend, not the way it's on the current site. **(required)**
7. Who are your customers? Who's the one you wish you had more of?
8. Who are your top two or three competitors, and what do you think they do better online than you?
9. Is there a site out there — competitor or not — that you look at and think "that's the feeling I want"? Link it.

---

## 2. What this site is for

10. If the new site does exactly one thing well, what is it? (get calls, take bookings, sell a product, look credible, collect leads) **(required)**
11. When someone lands on the site, what's the single action you most want them to take? **(required)**
12. How will you know in six months that it worked? What number moves? **(required)**
13. What's wrong with the current site, specifically? What makes you avoid sending people to it?

---

## 3. Where things stand today

14. Do you already own the domain name you want to use? **(required)**
    - If yes: what is it, and where did you buy it? (GoDaddy, Google Domains, Squarespace, Namecheap, someone set it up years ago and you're not sure, etc.)
    - If no: what would you *want* it to be? List a couple of options.
15. Is there a current site, and if so, what's it built on? (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, custom, no idea)
16. Who hosts it now, if you know?
17. What email addresses run on your domain? (you@yourcompany.com) Who provides that email — Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, something bundled with your host?
18. Do you have Google Analytics or any traffic data we can look at? Can you grant access?
19. Is there anyone who currently has the keys — a past developer, a nephew, an agency — that we'd need to coordinate with or get access from?

---

## 4. Pages and content

20. List the pages you think you need. First guess is fine. (Home, About, Services, Contact, etc.) **(required)**
21. Who is writing the words? Options:
    - You have copy already and it's ready
    - You have copy but it needs work
    - You want us to write it
    - Mix — mark which pages are which
22. Photos and logo:
    - Do you have a logo you're keeping? In what format? (PNG, SVG, "just a JPG someone made")
    - Do you have real photography of your team / work / space, or do we need stock?
    - Do you have brand colors and fonts already, or are we defining them?
23. Any legal or compliance content that has to appear? (privacy policy, terms, licensing, disclaimers)

---

## 5. Look and feel

24. Pick three words for how the site should feel. (e.g. calm, expensive, fast, friendly, serious)
25. Show us two or three sites you like and one you hate. Say why in a sentence each.
26. Are there brand guidelines we have to stay inside, or is this a fresh start?
27. Anything that's off the table? (colors you can't stand, a competitor's look to avoid)

---

## 6. What it needs to *do*

Check all that apply, and add detail where it matters.

- [ ] Contact form (where do submissions go?)
- [ ] Appointment / booking (which tool — Calendly, Acuity, etc.?)
- [ ] Online payments or store (how many products? which processor — Stripe, Shopify?)
- [ ] Blog or news / articles you'll update yourself
- [ ] A content system so you can edit pages without calling us (CMS)
- [ ] Newsletter / email capture (which provider — Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Resend?)
- [ ] Logins / member area / client portal
- [ ] Search
- [ ] Multiple languages
- [ ] Integrations with tools you already use — list them (CRM, accounting, scheduling, etc.)

28. Of everything checked above, which one is non-negotiable for launch, and which could come in a later phase?

---

## 7. Timeline and budget

29. Is there a hard date? (event, funding round, product launch, contract deadline) What is it and what's tied to it? **(required)**
30. What's the budget range you're working inside? An honest range helps us build the right thing instead of guessing. **(required)**
    - Under $3k · $3–5k · $5–10k · $10k+ · not sure, need guidance
31. Is this a one-time build, or are you expecting someone to keep it maintained and updated after launch?

---

## 8. How decisions get made

32. Who signs off on the final site? One person, or a committee? **(required)**
33. How fast can you turn around feedback and content? (this is usually what sets the timeline, not us)
34. Preferred way to work with us — email, a shared doc, a weekly call, Slack?

---

## 9. Beyond the website

You came to us for a site. That's the front door. But most of the businesses we
work with have a second thing quietly eating their week, and it's usually not the
website. A few questions so we know whether to bring it up — no pressure either way.

35. Outside of the website, where does your team lose the most time to manual, repetitive work? (copying data between tools, chasing spreadsheets, building the same report every month)
36. Are there two tools in your business that *don't* talk to each other and should? Which ones?
37. On the sales side: do you have a defined process, or does it live in people's heads? Do you know which stage deals tend to die at?
38. Is there a report, invoice, or task that a human rebuilds by hand on a regular schedule?
39. Would your team benefit from being *taught* to run some of this themselves, versus having us do it for you?
40. After launch, do you want someone on call for changes and monitoring, or are you handing it off completely?

> [Internal: answers here map straight to our service lines — operations audit,
> single-workflow and multi-system builds, sales framework/tracking/fractional,
> team training and custom skills, and care/fractional/embedded retainers. If
> two or more of Q35–Q40 light up, propose the two-week **operations audit** as
> the natural next step after the site ships. Don't pitch it in the same breath
> as the website quote; note it and raise it at handoff.]

---

## 10. Anything else

41. What haven't we asked that we should have?
42. What would make this project a clear win for you personally?
